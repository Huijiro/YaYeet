# VotV alpha 0.9.0l changelog

## Fixed

- A huge shadow covering half of the map.
- Miscellaneous overlays, including the underwater overlay and mirror mode.
- Data could not be deleted from the buffer when the inserted floppy had zero read/write capacity.
- The Ariral Easter egg could be eaten by Arirals.
- A pillar inside the locker.
- The crowbar created a "cannot pry" popup for objects that could not be pried.
- The Ariral ship was blocked by a tree.
- The signal panel FPS changed by itself.
- The maze minigame spawned 100% of the time.
- Bad sun could turn the player's camera when blocked by an object.
- Unlimited FPS on the disclaimer menu.
- The gas can did not snap to the cremator when placed with `R`.
- ATV wheel collisions with small objects.
- Grime could be cleaned too quickly.
- Breaking sounds caused by swinging doors had no distance falloff.
- The upgrade description window.
- The "Max capacity" popup on water guns.
- Easter bunny glitches.

## Changed

### Crowbar prying

- Adjusted the required force.
- Added snapping.

- Increased camcorder microphone volume.
- Reduced the amount of trash blobs.
- Adjusted wind ambience volume.
- Lights corresponding to coordinate towers now blink when the towers are broken.
- Added a notification to the Settings menu.

### Radio tower

- Added decorative elements.

- Adjusted UFO event timings.
- Signal pinging now calculates the correct probability.
- Added momentum back to cursors after switching.
- Added the ability to remove server upgrades.
- Disabled mushroom collisions.
- Updated the keybinds UI.

### Store UI

- Added a clear-search-prompt button.

- Trash clumps are now baggable.
- Rolling boulder dreams are now slower.
- Adjusted ATV audio volume.

## Added

- Three achievements.

## Hogs

- Overcharged.
